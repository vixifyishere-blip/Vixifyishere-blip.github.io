Rules

Know them before you play. Not knowing isn't an excuse.

Major rules

Breaking any of these gets you an immediate permanent ban. No warnings and no strikes.

  1. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.
  2. No threats, doxxing, or sharing anyone's personal information.
  3. No predatory or grooming behavior toward any member, especially minors.
  4. No NSFW, sexual, or graphic content.
  5. No encouraging self-harm or suicide.
  6. No cheating, hacked clients, duping, or exploiting bugs.
  7. No scamming, stealing from players, or posting malicious links.
  8. No raiding, impersonating staff, or any attempt to harm the server.
  9. No ban evasion or using alt accounts to dodge a punishment.

Minor rules

These earn a strike. Strikes stack up according to the system below.

  1. No spam, flooding chat, or excessive caps.
  2. No advertising other servers or sending unsolicited DMs.
  3. Keep arguments and trash talk friendly. No excessive toxicity.
  4. No excessive or targeted profanity.
  5. Use the right channels and keep bot commands in the bot channels.
  6. Don't abuse bot commands or economy features.
  7. Don't ignore or argue with staff instructions.
  8. No mini-modding. Report problems and let staff handle them.
  9. No minor trolling or pranks on the realm.

Strike system

Strike 1Warning
Strike 224-hour timeout
Strike 37-day ban
Strike 4Permanent ban

Strikes expire after 30 days with no new strikes. Staff may skip steps for repeated or deliberate violations.
